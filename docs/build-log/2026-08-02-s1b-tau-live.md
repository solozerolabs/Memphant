## Verdict

**NEGATIVE.** The preregistered rule required the latest-state-wins cluster
CI to exclude zero in the favourable direction. It excludes zero in the
harmful direction: the higher-precision threshold retires 361 fewer prior
units, improves hit at 10, and reduces the point estimate for
`neither_returned`, but loses 3.39 points of latest-state-wins and adds 4.05
points of misapplication. The watched `neither_returned` improvement is not
established because its interval touches zero.

Tau 0.42 remains the sentence-unit operating point. The favourable proof rule
was not met, so `docs/superpowers/specs/memphant/STATUS.md` is unchanged.
