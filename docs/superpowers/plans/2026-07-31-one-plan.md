## 1. The thesis: one root cause, measured four ways

Every headline failure traces to a single fact.

**No memory unit has a meaningful key.** `derive_fact_key`
(`crates/memphant-core/src/lib.rs:12331`) falls back to
`{scope}:auto:{sha256[..16]}` whenever subject and predicate are absent, and
nothing on the served path supplies them. On the preference corpus: **8,147
units, 0 predicates, 0 edges, 0 non-hot**.

That one fact produces all four of our worst results:

| Symptom | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Misapplication **0.6717** — two probes in three apply a *retired* rule | auto keys ⇒ `has_explicit_subject` false ⇒ the supersede branch at `lib.rs:11692` never runs ("AUTO-KEYS NEVER SUPERSEDE") |
| `MEMORY.md` is unreadable | `render_memory` (`file_plane.rs:1499`) titles links by `fact_key` — so our best-evidenced subsystem ships **a list of sha256 prefixes** |
| Assembly is built and unusable | `canonical_projection_units` (`store.rs:3701`) — a live, state-and-time-correct deterministic query — dedups and titles on `fact_key` |
| The bitemporal edifice is inert | correction rectangles, supersession edges, and the exclusion constraint at `bootstrap:833` all key on `fact_key`; **the constraint has never fired** |

**The mechanism to produce keys exists three times over, and all three are
unreachable.** A 450-line deterministic English extractor with negation handling
and last-occurrence-wins dedup (`service.rs:6185-6616`) is compiled in, tested,
and gated on `fact_extraction_enabled: false` (`service.rs:3454`) — whose only
caller in the entire tree is a benchmark (`bench_lme.rs:885`). A 4,258-line
LLM structured-state subsystem with typed slots, targeted supersession by prior
unit id, and exact-span verification sits dark behind
`MEMPHANT_STRUCTURED_STATE`. And caller-supplied `subject`/`predicate` exists in
the type but REST and MCP never set it.

**We have been losing to a lexical baseline while carrying three separate
mechanisms that would win, none of them plugged into the binary.**

### 1a. Two gates, not one — and two ranking defects that would mask any fix

Fixing the key is necessary and **not sufficient**. Four independent blockers:

- **Gate A** — `has_explicit_subject` false (the key problem above).
- **Gate B** — even *with* a key, the supersede scan (`lib.rs:11700-11706`)
  hardcodes `existing.kind == MemoryKind::Semantic`, and the shipped episode
  candidate is `Episodic` (`service.rs:5303`). **An Episodic unit can never be
  superseded by anything, ever.** A `Belief` or `Procedural` candidate with an
  explicit subject silently appends instead.
- **Defect 1 — `exact_score` divides by a UUID.** It tokenizes the whole
  `fact_key`, which is prefixed `{scope_id}:`; a hyphenated UUID contributes five
  constant junk tokens to every denominator, so a perfect match scores **0.25,
  not 0.667**. Today's `3fc4eede` multiplies the Exact contribution *by* that
  score, so **our own fix capped the channel at ~⅓ of its intended magnitude**.
- **Defect 2 — the recency channel ranks alphabetically.** `temporal_score`
  (`lib.rs:10918`) is binary: on any query containing `current`/`latest`/`now`,
  *every* Active Semantic unit scores exactly 1.0, then falls through to a
  `body.cmp(body)` tie-break. At weight 2.5 against Exact's 0.5, **Temporal beats
  Exact ~9× on precisely the "what is my current X" queries — by first letter.**

So a live rule and a dead one are currently separated by nothing, and then
ordered alphabetically. That is the whole preference-lane result.

## 2. What the evidence says we are wrong about

Recorded because each of these was, until today, a working belief.

1. **BM25 is not a control — it is near-random on our real task.** Three
   independent June-2026 groups: BM25 HitFile **0.079** on agentic code
   localization (arXiv:2606.07297); dense zero-shot collapses 71.7 → **20.3**
   NDCG@10 from code search to issue-to-edit (arXiv:2606.11864). Beating it by
   0.37 is beating a floor.
2. **Paraphrasing may be anti-correlated with the production task.** CORE-Bench
   found rewriting verbose issue text into concise queries sometimes *hurt* —
   identifiers, stack traces and error messages carry the signal, and our
   paraphrase bank strips exactly those.
3. **"Every margin is a lower bound" is inverted.** Margin *rises* as coverage
   falls (+0.0667 at 0.396 → +0.2389 at 0.1346). Paraphrase margins are **upper
   bounds**. And the bracket is internally inconsistent: it needs the original
   bank valid as an endpoint while the bias thesis needs it invalid.
4. **There was no packed sign flip.** The W0.2 arms were built on a branch
   lacking `f67f2b2a` and `3fc4eede`; their original-bank packed reference
   (0.7722) is pre-render-fix. Trunk is 0.9333, so that margin is **+0.0389, a
   win**. MemPhant wins packed on both banks.
5. **Deterministic keying caps at ~68%.** Third-party measurement of 13 systems
   (arXiv:2606.15903): deterministic primitives 63.4–68.3%, inscribe-time LLM
   **0%** on intent-aware deletion, mutation-time LLM hook **91.7–93.2%** at
   $0.17/385 mutations with the recall hot path untouched.
6. **Correction retention is enforcement, not retrieval.** Mem0 leaves **57.5%**
   of preference checks violated *with the preference in memory*
   (arXiv:2606.13174). Our LSW metric is wrong-shaped.
7. **Every LME number was measured in a config we do not ship** —
   `edge_expansion_enabled: false` served, `true` in every eval harness.
8. **Every "@10" on the chat slice is really @5** — max observed rank is 5.
9. **Our evidence-contract guards are inert.** `contracted: []`, so the checker
   validates nothing; and the flagship artifacts carry no decision keys, so they
   are invisible to it. Only `check_ci_workflow` is load-bearing.
10. **Provenance you can tap is false.** `source_span` exists end-to-end with real
    byte offsets and has **zero non-test consumers**.

## 3. The plan

Sequenced by information-per-dollar. **Nothing below Phase A starts until Phase A
reports** — that is the discipline the last three weeks lacked.

### Phase A — $0, days. Make measurement trustworthy, then make keys real.

**A1. Lineage stamping. Do this first, before any measurement.**
Record `git rev-parse HEAD` and a binary hash in the harness block every runner
already prints, and make lineage **the** field the evidence contract enforces.
Then re-run the four W0.2 arms on trunk. *Rationale: the program's flagship
result was measured two fixes behind its own trunk and nobody noticed. The
failure mode is not instrument bias — it is **lineage drift across nineteen
worktrees**, and thorough prose made an incompatible comparison look airtight.*

**A2. The three ~50-line ranking fixes.** Strip the scope UUID from
`exact_score`'s denominator (~3 lines). Make `temporal_score` a real recency
function of `valid_from`/`transaction_from` (~30 lines). Lift `kind == Semantic`
out of the supersede scan into the arm (~10 lines). *These move the number; the
router does not.*

**A3. Wire `fact_extraction_enabled` to server config, default ON.** One env
read. It is the only free thing that can move the preference number.

**A4. The oracle-key ablation — the highest-information experiment we have.**
Four arms on the frozen 1,063-probe / 257-cluster bank, all $0:
`A-oracle` (synthesize subject/predicate from MemoryCode's own labels),
`A-recency` (keys stay broken; add one `observed_at desc` tiebreak),
`A-projection` (answer from `canonical_projection_units`, unranked), against the
0.3123 baseline.

**This decides the architecture.** If A-oracle goes >0.7, keys are the whole game.
If **A-recency** wins, then twenty lines of recency beats the entire
correction-rectangle/bitemporal/supersession edifice on our #1 user pain, and the
honest response is deletion, not defence. *We have never measured recency because
we never implemented it.* That test is free and unrun.

**A5. Mechanism-liveness counters.** Every gate, bench and lever must emit and
assert a non-zero counter for the mechanism it claims to measure — `model_calls`,
`render_cap_applied`, `rerank_calls`, `jobs_drained == jobs_enqueued` — and fail
**before** any accuracy number is read. *Four of this week's six failures were
the same failure. `served_path_rls.rs:257-265` already does this by hand with a
live negative control; generalise that one pattern and the class closes.*

**A6. One `ensure_queue_clean`.** Six drain implementations exist; the shared one
never checks `dead`, and `code_lane_run_memphant.py:499` checks a **queue-wide
invariant with a tenant bound** — the exact shape FORCE RLS turned into a silent
zero. ~40 lines of deletion.

**A7. Three free validity probes.** Embedding-concentration on both banks (is the
dense flip real, or did we build a paraphrase-pair generator?); a third bank
mined inside the human band 0.175–0.287 (resolves the bounds question); and the
`--workspace`-floor guard kept as the one contract guard that is load-bearing.

### Phase B — ~$10–60, week two. Decide extraction, then compose.

**B1. Un-dark the structured-state extractor** and measure against A-oracle. It
already supersedes by naming exact prior unit ids, bypassing Gate A entirely, and
hard-rejects any evidence quote that does not reproduce its source span. ~$10–40
with cache replay. If it loses to A-oracle, the model cannot find the keys and
the answer is caller-authored keys instead.

**B2. Re-render frozen evidence as an assembled document** — grouped by subject,
deduped, superseded items *excluded* rather than ranked down — and run the paid
reader on byte-identical rows. Retrieval held constant; composition the only
variable. This is the cheapest paid experiment we have and it attacks the
measured composition bound directly.

**B3. Adopt ONCU's four conditions** (No-Evidence / Full-Context / Retrieved /
Oracle) in every lane. It turns "composition-bound" from an inference into a
measurement, and its No-Evidence arm is the contamination guard we keep needing.
Essentially free.

**W2.1 as preregistered does not run.** Chain-of-Note is a *reader prompt* — not
our substrate, and a gain there is one any consumer gets with any memory system.
Arm C (structured context rendering) is ours and folds into B2.

**`InMemoryStore` (~4,400 lines) is a decision, not a cleanup.** Its
`fetch_recall_candidates` **discards the query** and returns insertion order,
while Postgres runs three bounded families capped at 1,000. So every DB-free
recall and packing test measures a retrieval system that does not exist. Either
one shared conformance suite both stores must pass, or delete it and run
`with_scratch_db` everywhere — our own rule already says harnesses are ephemeral
Postgres. **I recommend deletion**: −4,400 lines, and it removes the seam rather
than testing it.

### Phase D — the product bet, once keys are real.

**D1. Emit a correction handle on every recall** —
`{unit_id, subject_generation, fact_key, valid_from, valid_to, source_span, episode_id}`
— and bind **Syndai's existing chip** to it. That UI already ships: provenance
chips under each assistant message, tap → *"Still true / Not anymore / Forget"*,
live-wired into mission chat, thirty locales. It currently reads
`syndai.memory_references`, and **`grep -ric memphant mobile/lib` returns
nothing**. A user tap becomes a **ground-truth supersession signal arriving
exactly where the stale rule was applied** — the cheapest correct signal
available, at the precise point of the 67% failure. This is the highest-leverage
UX change in the program and the consuming UI is already built.

**D2. Make the file plane legible.** Add `valid_from`/`valid_to` and
`source_span` to the unit footer (they are sent and discarded at
`file_plane.rs:1484`), and state/date/confidence columns to the `MEMORY.md`
index. ~50 lines. The projection is already *correct by construction* — it
filters state and both temporal intervals — so the moment supersession fires,
dead rules leave the file automatically. **The file plane is the primary human
surface for coding; a web dashboard is the wrong shape.**

**D3. Contradiction at write time, not abstention at read time.** Today an
unresolved contradiction makes recall abstain — the user gets nothing and learns
nothing. Instead write both units into `inbox/` as a two-line conflict and let
the human resolve it with a `sync`. Every mechanism exists. This is the
difference between silently holding a wrong opinion for three weeks and asking a
one-line question.

**D4. Measure against a harness's built-in memory.** We have never done it. Every
control has been BM25 or a lexical baseline — instruments we can beat. If
Claude Code's own memory matches us on retrieval, the niche is governance-only.
If it loses, that is the first number a buyer cares about.

## 4. Open questions — answered

**Hot/cold planes.** **Delete.** No feature, no instrument, no OSS precedent, and
the research says budget-driven eviction is the failure mode. `04` §13.3/§13.4
are withdrawn. If demotion returns, it is a recall-time predicate needing no
schema.

**OPEN-1, where compaction runs.** **Dissolved.** Compaction of working state is
dropping entries whose subject was superseded — set arithmetic, no LLM, the same
assembly primitive as `canonical_projection_units`.

**OPEN-2, `knowledge` as an enum rename.** **No.** It fills the same policy row as
`semantic`; it is the router-arm name.

**OPEN-3, a third always-injected hot slot.** **No.** B1 measured 0.000 paired
delta.

**OPEN-4, the unit-demotion mechanism.** **Moot** — tiering deleted.

**OPEN-5, cold episodes losing the dense channel.** **Moot.**

**OPEN-6, may a sub-agent write a user preference?** **Propose, never write.** It
writes to `inbox/` as a conflict; a human resolves it (D3).

**`working_state` as a kind.** **No new `MemoryKind` variant** — a namespace under
structured state. And reframe it: the load-bearing artifact is **negative
knowledge** ("tried X, failed because Y"), which is discrete and verbatim-quotable
and needs no summarization. Storing raw chain-of-thought invites
self-reinforcing hallucination.

**ANN index.** **Never, at this scale — and it is currently impossible anyway.**
Three structural blockers: `vec halfvec` has no dimension typmod so pgvector
cannot index it; the query has no matching cast; and the `, unit.body`
determinism tie-break forces a full sort and disqualifies index ordering. At 8k
units the vectors are 6–16 MB with recall 1.000, and HNSW's post-filter behaviour
is pathological against our heavily pre-filtered query — buying milliseconds with
recall, backwards for our priorities. **Record the decision in a comment on
`memphant.embedding`** naming the three changes and the volume trigger
(~500k rows/profile), because it will otherwise be re-litigated by someone
reaching for the obvious one-line fix that cannot work.

**Ownership question (d).** **Re-scoped to the convo lane. C1 is
correctness-only, permanently** — every human-turn↔memory join column is empty in
production, so the probe is structurally unbuildable there. The retrieval kill
gate does not fire, but **zero table migration**: replication, reader-QA, and
cutover are all outstanding.

**"No LLM at ingest."** **Amend.** It is the measured proximate cause of our worst
number — 0 predicates on 8,147 units. Keep the defensible core: **no LLM on the
*synchronous* write path; reflect stage 1 is the sanctioned seam.** Note that the
mutation-time control-plane hook (91.7–93.2%) sits at *mutation* time, not ingest,
and that caller-authored keys (D-next) make the question moot.

**"Verbatim is the memory."** **Keep, and enforce harder.** It costs nothing, and
`structured_state.rs:628`'s exact-span rejection is the right shape. State
explicitly that derived-alongside is permitted and rewritten-in-place is not.

**Extensions on managed Postgres.** The rule is the **intersection of Neon and
Supabase**, not "managed can't do extensions" — Supabase already has pgroonga,
rum, hypopg and index_advisor. `pg_search` stays out (AGPL) and `pg_textsearch`
stays out (`shared_preload_libraries`). `simple`-config tsvector + `pg_trgm` +
Rust BM25 is a legitimate substitute; we are not missing a BM25 extension we
could have had.

**Latency, cost, perf — the standing call.** Keep the exact vector scan. Move
`store_trace` **off the critical path** — it synchronously writes one record per
unit × channel (~6,000 records of JSON) before the caller sees a byte, and it is
an audit artifact, not part of the recall contract. Borrow instead of cloning
every unit per channel. Record `loadavg` and `cpu_count` beside every latency
figure in every runner — only one does today, which makes every other latency
number on this machine uninterpretable.

## 5. Standing rules — added

- **Lineage or it did not happen.** Every measurement artifact records the commit
  and binary hash it was produced from; the evidence contract enforces it.
- **Mechanism-liveness before accuracy.** A run that cannot prove the mechanism
  fired may not report a number.
- **A lane may add fields to provenance; it may never reimplement a primitive.**
  Enforced by AST duplicate detection in CI.
- **Calibrate a bar against a measured floor before preregistering it.** Three
  bars have now been set below what any answerable question can reach.
- **No paid authorization without a $0 adapter contract round-trip** since the
  last contract change. Three adapters failed at first contact; two after money
  was authorized; one would have billed $211–634 for zero rows.

## 7. A4 ANSWERED (2026-07-31, same day) — keys are the game, and producing them is the hard part

The oracle-key ablation ran. Verified independently from the arm artifacts:

| arm | LSW | misapplication | units | edges |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A — unchanged | 0.31232361 | 0.67168391 | 8,147 | **0 of any kind** |
| **P — oracle-keyed** | **0.57949200** | **0.34054563** | 14,088 | **7,198 supersedes + 3,599 contradicts** |
| F — `MEMPHANT_FACT_EXTRACTION` ON | 0.31232361 | 0.67168391 | 8,149 | 0 |

Δ LSW **+0.2672** [+0.2375, +0.2985]; Δ misapplication **−0.3311** [−0.3647, −0.2984];
0 open transactions on superseded rows.

> **RE-PINNED 2026-08-01 — the ceiling in this table is STALE. Do not divide by it.**
> Arm P's 0.57949200 was measured on another tree. Re-run on-tree at
> `main@5d7b9d5a` (`w1-arecency`, `baa267fa`, artifact `arm-bitemporal.json`,
> independently verified by `w1-keyprod`), the same oracle config scores
> **0.6237**. Mechanism counts reproduce exactly and misapplication is unchanged
> (0.3396 vs 0.3405); the whole **+4.4pp is retrieval coverage** (hit@k 0.840 →
> 0.9247), not staleness handling.
>
> **True headroom over A′ is +0.3114, not +0.2672** — anything computed as a
> "fraction of the oracle's headroom closed" against the old denominator
> **overstates itself by ~14% relative.** Three sessions were dividing by it.
>
> And the replacement is *equally* perishable: it moved 4.4pp in a day on
> coverage alone and has no reason to have stopped. Cite it as
> **"0.6237, measured 2026-08-01 on `5d7b9d5a`"** and re-cut it on whatever tree
> a claim is made against.
>
> **The general rule this earns (standing):** *if a quantity is not differenced
> within a single run, it has a shelf life.* Deltas survive base drift because
> they are properties of the **comparison** — both arms on one tree and one
> binary, so anything moving both absolute rates cancels. An absolute rate is a
> property of a tree, and every share computed from one inherits that. Δ +0.0301
> and Δ +0.0506 are durable; 0.6237 and "16.4% of headroom" are dated context.

**Three conclusions, and they redirect the plan.**

**1. The state machine works. It was never the problem — it was un-fed.** Given
real keys, supersession fires 7,198 times where it previously fired zero, LSW
nearly doubles and misapplication halves. The bitemporal edifice is **not
over-built**; §1's thesis is confirmed at full strength. This substantially
defuses the A-recency threat, though that arm remains worth running as the $0
control — the edifice is vindicated, not yet shown *necessary*.

**2. The binding constraint is producing the key, and it is genuinely hard.**
Measured, not assumed: a gold-independent body-derived key recovers **8 of 1,063**
gold groups (0.008); the best variant reaches 221/1,063 (0.208). Arm P is
explicitly `decisional: false` — its key comes from the same `topic` field the
gold does, so it is a ceiling, not a result. **The gap between 0.008 and that
ceiling is the actual work.**

**3. The flag was corpus-mismatched, not broken.** Arm F produced **2 semantic
units from 8,147 episodes**. `extract_facts` skips every non-`user` role and
matches first-person self-report; MemoryCode is second-person directives in
`Name: …` dialogue. The extractor is fine; this corpus is not its shape. That
does not retire it — it means the preference lane cannot test it, and the coding
and convo lanes must.

### Amendments to §3

- **A3 (wire the flag) — done, and it is a null on this lane.** Keep it default
  ON; measure it where the corpus matches.
- **A4 — closed.** Replaced by **A4′: make key production real.** That is now the

### §7's premise is AMENDED (2026-08-01, `w1-keyprod`)

**"The gap between 0.008 and that ceiling is the actual work" overstates the
problem, because 0.008 was measured under the wrong regime.** §4's figures
reproduce (18/224/124/60 vs the published 8/221/118/70) but were scored under
**one key per session, with every session in a group forced onto it** — while
Arm P mints one unit per *declaration* and a supersession edge points backwards
to *one* incumbent. Under the regime supersession actually uses, the same rule
family recovers **0.535–0.899**, and the honest floor is **net 0.514**
(`pre3_content_words`: net 0.5136 at 0.898 pair-precision, 44 golds lost).

That measurement's code had never been committed; it is now
`scripts/measure_key_recovery.py` with a permanent **constant-key control**
(recall 1.000, precision 0.043, 920 golds destroyed) so recovery can never again
be reported alone — §4 reported recall, which a constant key maximises perfectly.

**Do not interpolate key recovery into LSW.** Recovery is an *input* to LSW, not
LSW; multiplying them is the mismatched-stage error that voided a headline here
before. Arm K (`--arm derived`) is built and preregistered to measure it
directly, and is the free gate ahead of any paid work.

### B1 answered (2026-08-01, `w1-b1arm`) — positive mechanism, unproven semantics

Four full-bank arms, one tree, `paid_model_calls: 0`. **S − S0 = +0.0506**
[+0.0269, +0.0753], n_d 117 — the supersession edge pays, and S0 is a true no-op
isolator (identical 7,890 candidates, identical ledger, none named). But a
**rate-matched random arm that reads no bodies captures +0.0259 of that**, so
B1's *semantic* increment is **+0.0247, CI [−0.0033, +0.0532] — does not clear
zero.** Underpowered positive, not a null; the fix is more probes.

**The recency ablation nearly produced the opposite conclusion**, and the reason
generalises: **"recency" is two opposite policies.** Recency-as-*retirement*
closes the most recent live prior — but MemoryCode's gold IS the latest
declaring session, so that arm spends its whole edge budget on golds and lands
on a **floor**. Recency-as-*selection* (A-recency's control) retires nothing and
picks `max(observed_at)`, which computes the gold rule directly — a **ceiling**.
Only a *random* ablation asks the question. Without it this would have
overclaimed ~2×.

### A-recency answered (2026-08-01, `w1-arecency`) — the edifice earns its keep

**Δ LSW +0.0301** [+0.0170, +0.0443], **Δ misapplication −0.0245**
[−0.0376, −0.0125], n_d 62, McNemar p=5.78e-5, cluster-permutation p=1.00e-4.
Liveness passed two-sided (control at 0/0/0 with 10,294 overlapping open pairs).

**Keep the bitemporal machinery — but ~3 points is a margin, not a mandate**, and
it is measured against a *ceiling-shaped* baseline handed the answer's shape.
Weigh it against §5's exit price (one breaking migration). Scale-conditional:
live-row parity is exact at ~3.4 assertions per key and **does not transfer to
scopes carrying 200+ units per subject** without re-measuring or moving the
collapse into the store query.

collapsed into one headline:** the bitemporal *resolution* rule is worth ~3pp
